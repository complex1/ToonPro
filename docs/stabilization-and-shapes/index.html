Steady lines and hold-to-shape

Choose responsive smoothing, a stronger trailing stabilizer or explicit geometric constraints.

These instructions follow the current source build. Public 0.75 packages can differ.
In this chapterSmooth and StrongTune the stabilizerUse straight modifiersHold for a fitted shape

Smooth and Strong

The quick header offers Smooth and Strong tabs with Smoothing strength 0–100. Smooth applies distance-based low-pass filtering. Strong is the Stabilizer mode, with a longer pulled-string leash for deliberate inking. Both use logical view distance, including camera zoom, so the setting has a consistent working-view scale. Higher strength adds lag and can suppress small details.

Tune the stabilizer

Advanced → General exposes Dead zone 0–20 logical view pixels and Finish stroke. Dead zone ignores small motions in Stabilizer mode. Finish follows retained smoothed history in order on release; off stops at the last stabilized tip. These preferences are saved per tool. Changes made during contact take effect on the next stroke.

  1. Try Smooth 20–50 for rough gestures.
  2. Try Strong 50–70, dead zone 1–2 and Finish on for deliberate contours.
  3. Reduce strength for lettering or tight repeated loops.
  4. Use an exact guide when the goal is a precise ellipse or perspective line.
Keep in mind

These are starting points, not measured optima for your tablet. Synthetic attenuation fixtures do not certify device latency.

Use straight modifiers

Hold Command/Ctrl before contact for horizontal/vertical brush or raster eraser strokes; the first clear movement chooses the artwork axis. Hold Shift before contact for an adjustable arbitrary-angle line from start to current endpoint. The modifier mode is captured at contact. Guide snapping and freehand stabilization are bypassed for these constrained strokes; current brush resources and pressure still contribute.

Hold for a fitted shape

Draw a line, arc, circle, ellipse, rectangle or triangle, keep contact and pause for 0.7 seconds. If a shape fits, a clean preview appears. Lift and choose a thumbnail or Original; Cancel/Escape discards the stroke. Moving before release returns to freehand. Hold to shape has a remembered on/off wand toggle. It does not guarantee a match for arbitrary scribbles or add a post-fit node-handle system.
