Layers / LOCAL 0.76 SOURCE

Masks, filters and live outlines

Hide artwork nondestructively and compose blur, shadow, glow, color correction and silhouette borders.

These instructions follow the current source build. Public 0.75 packages can differ.
In this chapterOpen EffectsPaint a maskChoose an effectOutline the silhouetteProcessing and scope

Open Effects

Select an artwork layer/folder and open Panels → Effects. Mask, Blur, Shadow, Glow, Color and Outline are direct category tabs. Header bypass retains stored color/lighting/outline settings; masks have their own enable switch. Controls preview live and document-backed drags commit once. Effects run through the shared Canvas, Navigator and export compositor.

Paint a mask

Add a white mask or create one from the active selection. Paint Hide/Reveal with dedicated round Size, Strength and Hardness controls; pressure affects strength and selection coverage applies. Invert, reveal all, hide all, disable and remove are direct actions. Escape cancels an active mask stroke or exits idle mask painting. Mask Placement controls move, scale and rotate its source independently; opacity adjusts strength and feather softens coverage.

Choose an effect

Blur uses a three-pass box approximation of Gaussian blur. Shadow adds a colored offset silhouette; its pad controls X/Y alongside exact values. Glow creates a halo. Color offers linear-light exposure plus contrast, saturation and hue on unassociated sRGB color while retaining alpha. Adjustment layers apply supported color correction to lower siblings in their folder.

Outline the silhouette

Outline adds a live outer border following rendered alpha, including holes. Width is 0–128 source pixels, with color selection and a 3-pixel white default. Changing paint updates the border; original raster/vector/text sources remain editable. A folder outlines its visible combined children. An opaque photograph receives a rectangular border unless its background is removed.

Processing and scope

Processing order is mask → color → blur → outline → shadow/glow → opacity/blend/clipping. Masks hide content before outlining; lighting therefore includes the border. Direct Notes/3D-reference/adjustment outline controls are unavailable. Outline width/color are static; no inside/center alignment, watercolor border or dedicated outline animation exists. Other supported effect/mask scalar channels can be animated in an animation document.
