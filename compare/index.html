KIND OF FLOW.

The right workspace depends on what you make. Here is where Toon Pro fits alongside established creative tools.

Why this direction?

01

Start with one drawing.

Create a focused drawing project before thinking about shots or a whole production.

02

Keep organization separate.

Projects and assets live in their own dashboard; independent documents open in tabs.

03

Keep the work local.

Project-owned media, native editable documents and ordinary folders on your selected drive.

Illustration, comics and 2D animation

Clip Studio Paint

Official product documentation ↗

Where it shines

A mature art workflow with customizable brushes, vector layers and 3D drawing references. Animation capacity depends on the edition.

Where Toon Pro fits

Toon Pro’s direction is a drawing-first native editor with a separate project dashboard and independent document tabs. Full Clip Studio feature parity remains unfinished.

Digital painting and frame-by-frame animation

Krita

Official product documentation ↗

Where it shines

A mature painting application with a configurable brush engine, animation timeline and audio support.

Where Toon Pro fits

Toon Pro combines a local project/asset manager, editable vector drawing and shot documents. Krita has a much more established painting ecosystem.

2D animation production

OpenToonz

Official product documentation ↗

Where it shines

Raster and vector drawing, Xsheet/timeline exposure workflows and node-based effects for animation production.

Where Toon Pro fits

Toon Pro starts with an individual drawing and keeps organization outside the canvas. Complete multi-shot assembly and delivery remain open work.

Production animation and compositing

Toon Boom Harmony

Official product documentation ↗

Where it shines

A professional animation family with drawing, rigging and compositing tools. Availability depends on the edition; the Node view is a Premium feature.

Where Toon Pro fits

Toon Pro’s current priority is direct drawing UX for an individual artist. It does not claim Harmony’s rigging, production pipeline or node compositor depth.

3D creation and Grease Pencil animation

Blender

Official product documentation ↗

Where it shines

A broad 3D creation suite; Grease Pencil supports traditional 2D, cut-out animation and storyboarding in a 3D environment.

Where Toon Pro fits

Toon Pro uses editable 3D primarily as a drawing reference. Full 3D creation, advanced rendering, physics and general scene authoring are outside its current scope.

A comparison, with context.

These are workflow distinctions, not measured speed or quality rankings. Toon Pro remains in development. Physical pen acceptance, broad platform packaging, large-project behavior, live linked assets and full multi-shot delivery still need work. Other products are mature and have different strengths.
