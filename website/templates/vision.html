WHO IT IS FOR

THE ARTIST
AT THE CANVAS.

A beginner deserves a clear first step. An experienced artist deserves depth within reach.

The core audience is independent illustrators, animators and small creative teams exploring hand-drawn anime. Some begin with a sketchbook. Some already know painting tools and want to animate. Some need a place to organize the many pieces of a story.

The aim is a workflow that grows with those needs: direct creative work first, with organization and more advanced controls available as the project asks for them.

THE PRINCIPLES

A FEW THINGS
WE KEEP COMING BACK TO.

01

Start with the work.

A drawing begins with a name, a canvas and a background. Optional production organization comes around that simple beginning.

02

Keep one moment in focus.

The new animation editor represents one shot. Movie organization belongs in the project dashboard, where it can support the drawing.

03

Keep the source editable.

Native drawings and animations retain their layers and timing when moved into Assets. Imported media is copied into the project.

04

Make the controls explain themselves.

Recognizable tools, direct visual controls, exact entry and practical guides help the artist understand the next action.

05

Keep the artist in charge.

The app supports deliberate drawing, timing and finishing decisions. Reference images and illustrative website art are identified for what they are.

06

Earn the production claim.

Real projects, physical tablet testing, platform validation and dependable delivery have to establish production readiness over time.

HONEST EXPECTATIONS

AN AMBITION
WITH THE DETAILS IN VIEW.

Toon Pro is a developing product. Its new Movie dashboard organizes work; it is not a completed movie-editing, review-and-approval or studio collaboration system. Large-production memory budgets, every tablet/driver combination and cross-platform delivery still require dedicated acceptance.

Public source availability does not establish a license or a price. Application licensing and pricing are undecided. This website does not promise a performance lead, seamless round trips with other creative tools, or production acceptance that has not been demonstrated.
