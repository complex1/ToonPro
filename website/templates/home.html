A first drawing deserves a clear beginning. A bigger story deserves room to grow.

Toon Pro brings drawing, frame-by-frame animation and artist references into a desktop workspace. The new project flow adds organization around that canvas: drawings when you want to draw, a single shot when you want to animate, and folders when your story gets bigger.

03 / A SMALL IDEA, IN MOTION

WHAT IS
ONE SHOT?

One canvas. One continuous moment.

A blink, a turn, a paper plane drifting past a window. Start there. Draw the key poses, hold a drawing for a few frames, then play the moment back.

The example shows six poses across twelve frames. Select a frame or press Play to see how a held drawing becomes motion.
